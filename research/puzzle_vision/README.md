# Puzzle vision
## Naming convention
`face` - one side of cube: U, R, F, D, L or B
`facelet` - fixed place on face, not associated with color, after cube move facelet stays in place and (likely) changes color
`facelets` - Kociemba's cube state format, 54 leters URFDLB, extend in this application with unknown color "-"
`color` - the color of facelet or face, face has color of its center
`sticker` - polygon of color on photo (annotation)
